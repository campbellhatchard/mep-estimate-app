# CIP New Client SOW UAT

1. Create a CIP estimate with Customer Type = Net New and Project Type = CIP Install.
2. Confirm Calculation Data contains CIP SOW Setting / Current Version and set the desired current CIP production version through the normal controlled configuration lifecycle.
3. Configure representative scope: Gateway, optional EPP, applications, integrations/REST, labels, security and Go-Live Support.
4. Submit and approve the estimate.
5. Open SOW and Prepare SOW. Confirm CIP Product Version is inherited from the estimate's pinned configuration and is read-only.
6. Complete the applicable Appendix A fields, printer count, devices and Hypercare allocation. Hypercare hours must exactly reconcile to approved CIP Go-Live Support hours.
7. Confirm PDF review includes only approved/selected scope and excludes Limited Load Test for Net New CIP while the calculation has zero `TEST_LIMITED_LOAD` hours.
8. Finalize, assign a different SOW Approver and Send for Approval.
9. Approver reviews the PDF from Approvals and either rejects with a required reason or approves.
10. After approval, download Word and verify cover, TOC, X.0/X.Y/X.Y.Z numbering, page starts, headers/footers, commercial values, Terms/signatures and Appendix A.
11. Confirm CIP Change, EPP-only and Small Project estimates do not offer the CIP New Client SOW.
12. Confirm existing MEP SOW preparation, approval, PDF and Word output remain unchanged.
